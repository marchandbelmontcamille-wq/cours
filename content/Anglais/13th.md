# 13th November 2015 - Paris Attacks

## Context

At that time, France was fighting the terrorist group called **ISIS** in Syria and Iraq. The French army was taking part in **airstrikes**.

In reaction, ISIS targeted France, not only because of the military actions, but also because of what France represents: **freedom and democracy**.

## The attacks

- The attackers didn't choose **symbols of power**, but **places of everyday life**.
- Their goal was to attack **daily French activities**.
- They attacked **French values and ways of living**.
