In the 2008 democratic primaries in New Hampshire, Barack Obama gave a speech that because known as the "yes we can" speech.

He was the first afro-american man who could really win the presidential election (versus the republican McCain).

The strong enthusiasm provoked by Obama at the time was called "Obamania". He was not only charismatic, young, a great orater but above all he was a symbol of progress in the American black history. Indeed, only 40 years earlier, segregation was abolished and MLK was assassinated.

This speech put an emphasis on hope/change/unity/american values/equality.

Obama was finally elected in november 2008 as the 44$^{th}$ american president.
