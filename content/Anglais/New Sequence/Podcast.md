**Why we should remember the Stolen Generations:**

- This episode caused a lot of trauma (abuse, psychological issues, addictions) that are passed down from generation to generation
- Many Aboriginal languages, traditions and customs were lost because they weren't taught to the next generation
- Inequalities between Indigenous people and the rest of the population still exist (life expectancy, poverty, education)
- In 2008, the Prime Minister apologised but many people think it's not enough
- Sorry Day (May 26th) is celebrated every year to remember
- It's a duty of memory to prevent it from happening again, which is good for society

**Debate: Should Australia leave the Commonwealth?**

- Full sovereignty: No more British monarch as head of state
- End of colonialism: Imperial legacy rejected by Aboriginal people
- Elected president: More democratic and local institutions
- Popular majority: Around 60% of Australians want a republic
