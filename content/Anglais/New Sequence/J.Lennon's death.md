## 6) The aftermath and Lennon legacy

Lennon was shooted on the 8$^{th}$ of Dec 1980 in NYC.
On that day, he left in the morning with his wife Yoko Ono for a photo shoot and they went home, to the Dakota Building where a fan asked for an autograph . After spending the rest of the day in a record studio, he was shot 4 times by the very same fan around 10 pm. The shoot, Mark David Chapman, a 25 year old man, who they remained very calm as the police intervined and took Lennon to the Roosevelt Hospital. His injuries were very serious and the doctors tried to save his live for 30 minutes without knowing his identity. When realising who had just died, the doctor talked of a "omg moment". After his death, fans gathered and sang his songs. In 1985, Yoko Ono and NYC mayor created "Strawberry Fields" area in Central Park, dedicated to Lennon. Today, Lennon's legacy carrie on in his songs and his message.
