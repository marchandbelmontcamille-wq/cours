## Notes pour les questions
### 3)
- Accept Commonwealth Charter (2013) : democracy, human rights, rule of law, good governance
- Apply → approved by all existing members
- Usually : historical/constitutional link to a current member (ex: former British colony)
- BUT not mandatory anymore (ex: Mozambique 1995, Rwanda 2009 → no British colonial past)
- Accept the King/Queen as symbolic Head of the Commonwealth
- Can be a republic, a monarchy, or any system of government
- Members can leave and rejoin (ex: South Africa left 1961, rejoined 1994)

- Voluntary association → no country is forced to stay
- No binding laws → not like the EU, no common parliament or legislation
- Gather 56 countries and 2.5 billion people → very diverse (small islands + large nations)
- Mix of rich and poor countries, all treated as equals
- Common working language: English
- Focus on cooperation, not control
- Head of Commonwealth role is not automatic/hereditary (They choose King Charles III in 2018)
### 4)
- Originally: CW members had to recognize the British monarch as their Head of State
- After WWII → wave of independence movements, many countries wanted to become republics
- Problem: if they become republics → no more allegiance to the Crown → can they stay in the CW?
India :
- India = first big test case
- Wanted to become a republic but also stay in the Commonwealth
- Solution → **London Declaration 1949**: members can be republics, just accept the monarch as **symbolic "Head of the Commonwealth"** (≠ Head of State)
- Huge change: shifted CW from "British club" to a more equal association
Consequences :
- Opened the door for many African & Asian nations to join as republics
- South Africa = example of tensions → became republic 1961, left CW (because of apartheid, other members opposed it) → rejoined only in 1994
- Today: most CW members are actually republics (36 out of 56)
- Only 15 countries still have the King as Head of State ("Commonwealth realms")
- Transformed the CW from colonial structure → voluntary, equal partnership
- Without this change, the CW would probably not exist anymore today
