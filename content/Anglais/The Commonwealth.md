## 1. The Empire
The British Empire was a group of colonies ruled by britain. At its peak (climax), it covered 1 quarter of the world. Colonies were a real asset as they provided resources and markets

The British Empire was born in the 16$^{th}$ century and reached its peak at the end of the 19$^{th}$/early 20$^{th}$
It was a global super power based on its :
- military power
- economics
- cultural influence
After WWII, movements of self determination emerged in colonies, and they all started to gain independance for self determination
Today, cultural influence is still very much present in former colonies
## 2. The end of Empire
### A. Why decolonization ?
After 1945, differents factors pushed Britain to give independance to its colonies. First, Britain was economically weak after WWII and could no longer afford a large empire.
Then, many colonised peoples started nationalist movements and demanded freedom undeed protests.
Finally, the international Comminity (U.N) criticized colonialism and supported independance.
Therefore, because of their 3 reasons, decolonisation became unevitable
